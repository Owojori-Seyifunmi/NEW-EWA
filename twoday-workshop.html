Why this workshop

Property can work harder when the strategy is clearer.

Many investors are dealing with inconsistent rent, unclear demand, rising costs and too many unanswered questions. This two-day blueprint gives you a structured way to understand where social housing opportunities fit, what makes a deal viable and how to move forward with confidence.

01

Understand the social housing opportunity

See why demand exists, who the key players are and how ethical property provision can become a practical investment route.

02

Learn how to assess deals properly

Break down location, provider fit, lease structure, compliance needs, refurbishment costs and realistic net returns.

03

Build a plan you can act on

Leave with a clearer plan for sourcing, analysing, structuring and progressing opportunities after the workshop.

The blueprint

What you will work through across the two days.

01

Market and demand

Understand the role of social housing, supported living and local demand before you look at properties.

02

Deal criteria

Learn what makes a property suitable, what to avoid and how to assess risk before moving forward.

03

Lease structures

Explore lease-based routes, provider expectations and the commercial logic behind longer-term rental models.

04

Numbers and cashflow

Review refurbishment, setup costs, rent expectations, margins and the difference between gross and net outcomes.

05

Funding routes

Understand ways investors approach opportunities, including creative structures and how to think about capital.

06

Execution plan

Map your next steps, including sourcing, due diligence, provider conversations and follow-up actions.
