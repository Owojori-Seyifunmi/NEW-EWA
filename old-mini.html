Why Make The Shift

Social Housing Isn't Just Safer. It's Smarter.

While other landlords are selling up or scrambling to keep up with legislation, savvy investors are quietly pivoting, and building recession-proof income streams in the process.

Government-Backed Income

Rents paid directly by local authorities and housing associations. No arrears. No chasing. No void periods.

Long-Term Leases

Contracts typically run 3 to 10 years with built-in rent reviews. The stability of a commercial lease with the demand of residential housing.

Higher Net Yields

Social housing and supported living typically generates 10 to 25%+ net yields, significantly above standard buy-to-let.

Renters Rights Bill Proof

Social housing leases are held with providers, not individual tenants. The Renters Rights Bill largely doesn't apply, so you're protected.

Ethical & Values-Led

Generate real wealth while solving a genuine social problem. Your property does good in the community, and delivers exceptional returns at the same time.

Massive Unmet Demand

The UK needs 90,000+ supported living units. Demand from councils and housing providers is outpacing supply. The opportunity window is wide open.
